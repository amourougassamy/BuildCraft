BuildCraft

Hackathon iterations. Each folder is a complete, playable version; newest first.

Team mainline

  • master v1 Master version: the best of every prototypelatest
    Kid-first and claymation-style 3D. Type your name, pan the island map, and unlock 5 sites. Every choice is on one screen with a coin jar. The year runs forward only: people walk in with emoji moods, you tap to repair, and closed days count as downtime. Ghosts, eco points, an A+ report card and a game-over screen. Works offline.     Play
  • v0.2 Kid edition: the regulars move in
    Toy-box look, picture cards, coin jar, six characters with happy / sad / ghost moods, celebrations, Gentle mode, sound.     Play
  • v0.1 Starter: Riverside Library
    One brief, Minneapolis weather, 9 decisions, demo year and random year.     Play

Prototypes

  • tycoon Building Tycoon: every discipline in one buildingTom
    Isometric 3D office you can orbit. Tenants come and go; their mood follows the design. Bigger windows heat the place up and weaken the shear walls; a helicopter flies in rooftop units, which load the roof and the electrical service. A year of heat, cold, wind, snow and a quake. Needs internet for Three.js.     Play
  • alex Towns, soils and surprise quakesAlex
    Pick a town, building type and floors. 90-second build, then a 12-month year of earthquakes and windstorms; foundations must match the soil. Earlier v1 and v2 are in the same folder.     Play
  • sky-high Sky High Builders: the wind challengeLogan
    Stack pieces on a grid to build a tower for a windy city.     Play
  • 3d BuildCraft 3DLogan
    Orbitable 3D tower, house, bridge and frame templates with six adjustable loads and a demand/capacity score. Needs internet for Three.js.     Play
  • next.js Neighborhood learning hubnabdo
    3D design against a budget, an area target and a hot-day cooling simulation. Hosted on Abacus.AI; source in prototypes/learning-hub-nextjs/.     Open
